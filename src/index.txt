Index of the src/ directory:

addons/ - Example sources for AddonC server-side plugins. Chat sounds, banners etc.
botlib/ - isolates Multiplayer Bot AI
client/ - basis for the csprogs.dat
common/ - Included into most components
gs-entbase/ - Implementations of historical entity classes
menu-fn/ - Menu inspired by the original 1998 WON menu used in HL
menu-vgui/ - Our modern Vera GUI menu
nav/ - Code related to routing/nav-mesh. Used by server-entities and botlib
platform/ - Basis for menu.dat
server/ - Basis for progs.dat
shared/ - Shared by csprogs.dat and progs.dat
vgui/ - Vera GUI objects and classes

addon.src = Include this into your AddonC progs
app.src = Include this into your AppC progs
hud.src = Include this into your HudC progs
rules.src = Include this into your RuleC progs.
